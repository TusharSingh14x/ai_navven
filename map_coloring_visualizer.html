The 5-Wheel Graph Bottleneck ($W_5$)

Why India can never be colored with 3 colors:

  • Central Hub: Madhya Pradesh (MP) borders 5 states in a contiguous ring.
  • Outer 5-Cycle ($C_5$): Gujarat (GJ) → Rajasthan (RJ) → Uttar Pradesh (UP) → Chhattisgarh (CG) → Maharashtra (MH) → GJ.
  • Mathematical Constraint: Any odd cycle requires at least 3 colors to avoid adjacent conflict.
  • Hub Impossibility: MP touches every single vertex in this 5-cycle, so MP must have a color different from all of them, forcing a 4th color ($3 + 1 = 4$).
